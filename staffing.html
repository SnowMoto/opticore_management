Recruiting & Staffing Project Management

At Opticore, we provide project management solutions designed to meet you where you are. Whether you need temporary support, scalable leadership, or a long-term strategic partner — our engagement models adapt to your unique goals, timelines, and budget.

On-Demand:

Short-term, high-impact project support. Ideal for urgent initiatives or targeted leadership — pay only for what you need, when you need it.

Contract:

Longer-term engagements (typically 3+ months) with defined milestones and KPIs. A dedicated PM without the cost of a full-time hire.

Fractional:

Part-time leadership on a consistent weekly basis — often 3–6 months. Scalable, cost-effective, and deeply integrated with your team.

Strategic Project Staffing

Fractional Project Management

Fractional project management gives you experienced leadership, with smarter tracking and lower costs, on demand – only when you need it. It’s a guaranteed time frame that is a Lean and flexible way to get projects moving without the burden of full-time staffing.

Instead of hiring a full-time project manager or bringing on expensive contractors, you get dedicated flexible, on-demand access to a skilled professional who can guide your projects, track performance, and keep things on schedule — all at a fraction of the cost.
